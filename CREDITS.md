# Aurethra — credits & asset licenses

Aurethra is free forever. Every premade asset in it is free and openly licensed
(CC0 first). Each pack is listed here with its source and license. Rule:
docs/DESIGN.md §14.

## Visual effects
- **PVFX Foundry 1.0** by nerijs / Pixel VFX Studio — https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry
  License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication; see `fx/pvfx/LICENSE.txt`).
  101 animated effect sprite sheets ("lit" tier, grid layout) in `fx/pvfx/`.
  Imported with `tools/art/import-pvfx.mjs`. "VFX from PVFX Foundry by Pixel VFX Studio."
  (The pack's author notes AI assisted the development of their rendering engine; the owner approved its use.)

## Sound effects
- **RPG Sound Pack** by artisticdude — https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
  License: **CC0**. 30 of its 95 sounds are in `sfx/rpg/` (swings, spells, clashes, UI,
  coins, potions, creature voices).
- Heartbeats, Heart Moment stingers, break and Heartbind hits are synthesized in code
  (`src/game/sfx.ts`).

## Original / generated
- Party, enemy, resident sprites and tiles: generated by `tools/art/` from the owner's concept art.
- Files listed in `art/HANDMADE.txt` are the owner's own paintings.
